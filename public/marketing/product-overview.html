Core Features

📸
ALPR Integration
Automatic license plate recognition using Plate Recognizer API. Scan plates with your phone camera and get instant vehicle details, compliance status, and historical data. Supports both manual and high-speed scanning modes.
🤖
AI-Powered Analysis
OnSpace AI automatically detects vehicle make, model, color, and self-contained status from photos. Smart pattern recognition identifies repeat offenders and compliance trends without manual data entry.
📍
GPS Zone Tracking
Geofenced zone management with automatic GPS-based vehicle assignment. Track overnight stays using consecutive-day detection algorithm. All photos watermarked with GPS coordinates and timestamps for court evidence.
⚖️
Compliance Engine
Automated compliance evaluation against zone-specific rules (nights per month, consecutive stays, self-contained requirements). Freedom Camping Act exemption logic for confirmed homeless vehicles. Real-time breach detection.
🚨
Breach Management
Automatic breach alert generation with workflow flexibility (officer-first or admin-first approval). Track enforcement actions (warnings, notices, tow requests) with delivery confirmation and follow-up scheduling.
📊
Analytics Dashboard
Real-time analytics with zone heatmaps, compliance trends, officer performance metrics, and exportable reports for council meetings. BI-style visualizations for data-driven decision making.
👥
Officer Welfare
Built-in safety features with GPS tracking, auto-logoff warnings, inactivity alerts, and escalation protocols. Real-time officer location monitoring for patrol supervisors and emergency response.
📱
Mobile-First Design
Progressive Web App (PWA) optimized for field officers. Works offline with automatic sync, native camera integration, and one-handed operation. Install as app on iOS and Android devices.
🔍
Investigation Jobs
Create and assign investigation tasks for homeless occupation, abandoned vehicles, and trespass incidents. GPS-watermarked evidence photos, police integration tracking, and court-ready reporting.

Field Officer Workflow

See how FC Manager streamlines daily patrol operations from scan to enforcement

1
Select Zone & Start Patrol
Officer logs in, selects patrol zone (e.g., "Cromwell - Lowburn Ferry Reserve"), and checks in with GPS verification. System displays zone rules and current compliance stats.
2
Scan Vehicles (ALPR)
Officer uses phone camera to scan license plates. System instantly recognizes plate (e.g., "ABC123"), retrieves vehicle history, and evaluates compliance against zone rules. AI detects vehicle make/model/color automatically.
3
Automatic Compliance Check
Backend calculates: nights stayed this month (8/28), consecutive nights (2/3), self-contained status (expired), homeless exemption (not applicable). Result: COMPLIANT or BREACH DETECTED.
4
Enforcement Action (If Needed)
For non-compliant vehicles: Officer chooses action (verbal warning, written notice, tow request). System generates legal notice with GPS evidence, sends email to registered owner, and schedules follow-up patrol.
5
Report & Analytics
All data automatically synced to cloud. Admins see real-time dashboards with zone heatmaps, compliance trends, and officer productivity. Export reports for council meetings with one click.
